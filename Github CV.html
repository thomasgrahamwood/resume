About

I run digital marketing end to end: SEO and AI search optimization, HubSpot and lead nurturing, paid social, and content across blog, newsletter, video and podcast. At Intersec, a public-safety software company serving governments and telecom operators, I am one half of a two-person marketing team, so I handle both the plan and the hands-on execution on every channel.

I believe the solution matters more than the sector. If you genuinely care about a product and understand the people it serves, you can market it well anywhere. Going deep in a technical niche taught me that strong marketing comes from curiosity and rigor.

LLMs like ChatGPT and Claude are part of how I work every day. I use them to pressure-test ideas, challenge my own drafts and move faster from brief to delivery, so more of my time goes into strategy and less into repetitive tasks.
